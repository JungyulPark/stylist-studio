## Viral Campaign Planning

### Discount Code Strategy (70% Off Campaigns)

**When to deploy 70% discount codes:**
- Product launch moments (new feature = new campaign)
- Holiday periods (New Year makeover, summer style, back-to-school)
- Influencer partnerships (unique codes per influencer)
- Re-engagement for churned users

**Code naming convention:**
- Influencer codes: `[NAME]70` (e.g., `JENNA70`)
- Seasonal codes: `[SEASON][YEAR]` (e.g., `SUMMER26`)
- Feature launch: `[FEATURE]LAUNCH` (e.g., `HAIRLAUNCH`)
- Flash sales: `FLASH[NUMBER]` (e.g., `FLASH70`)

**Campaign structure:**
1. **Pre-launch** (2 days): Teaser content, "something big is coming"
2. **Launch day**: Announce code across all platforms, influencer posts go live
3. **Urgency phase** (days 2-4): Countdown, "only X hours left"
4. **Last call** (day 5): Final push, show results from people who already used it
5. **Post-campaign**: Share results, thank community, tease next campaign

### Referral Amplification

The app has a built-in referral system (6-character codes). Marketing should:
- Incentivize sharing: "Share your code, both you and your friend get a free styling"
- Create shareable referral graphics/templates
- Feature top referrers on social media
- Run referral competitions: "Most referrals this month wins [prize]"

### UGC (User-Generated Content) Campaigns

- Branded hashtag challenges: #MyAIGlowUp
- Monthly "Best Transformation" contest
- Repost user results (with permission) on brand accounts
- Create a "Wall of Transformations" landing page with real user before/afters

### Ad Creative Guidelines

1. **Hook format**: Always lead with transformation result, then reveal it was AI
2. **Video length**: 15-30 seconds for TikTok/Reels, 30-60 seconds for YouTube
3. **CTA clarity**: "Try it free" outperforms "Learn more" by 3x
4. **Social proof**: Include "4.8/5.0 satisfaction" or user count when available
5. **A/B test**: Always run 3-5 creative variants per campaign

### Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | Tool |
|--------|--------|------|
| CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) | < $2.00 | Ad platform |
| ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) | > 3x | Ad platform + Polar |

## Chatbot Monetization ($0.99 Styling Consultation)

### Concept

A conversational AI styling assistant that provides personalized style advice for $0.99 per session.

### User Flow

1. User sees "Chat with Your Stylist" CTA on result page or landing page
2. Opens chat interface (in-app or separate page)
3. First 2 messages free (hook), then paywall: "Unlock full consultation for $0.99"
4. After payment: 20-minute session with follow-up questions
5. Session ends with actionable style summary + save to profile

### Conversation Topics

- "What colors suit my skin tone?"
- "Help me build a capsule wardrobe"
- "What should I wear to [specific event]?"
- "How do I transition my style from casual to professional?"
- "What accessories would complete this outfit?"

### Implementation Notes

- Use GPT-4 with system prompt trained on styling expertise
- Include user's photo analysis data as context (face shape, skin tone, body proportions)
- Maintain conversation memory within the session
- Generate one complimentary outfit image at end of session (upsell to full package)

### Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Quick Question | $0.99 | 5-minute chat, 1 recommendation |
| Full Consultation | $2.99 | 20-minute chat, 3 outfit images |
| Monthly Advisor | $6.99/mo | Unlimited chat + daily style (bundled with subscription) |

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Core Personality

**Confident but approachable.** We are the stylish friend who always knows what works -- not a snobbish fashion critic. We empower, not intimidate.

### Tone Attributes

| Attribute | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| Confident | "This hairstyle will transform your look" | "This might possibly work for some people" |
| Inclusive | "Every face shape has its perfect match" | "Only oval faces look good with..." |
| Modern | "AI-powered styling in 30 seconds" | "Our algorithm computationally processes..." |
| Warm | "Your glow-up starts here" | "Purchase our product" |
| Direct | "Upload. Style. Shine." | "If you would like to perhaps consider..." |

### Language Rules

1. **No jargon**: Say "face shape" not "craniofacial morphology"
2. **Active voice**: "AI styles your hair" not "Your hair is styled by AI"
3. **Short sentences**: Max 15 words for headlines, 25 for body copy
4. **Positive framing**: "Find your perfect style" not "Stop looking bad"
5. **No emojis in product UI**: Use clean typography and icons instead (emojis are for social media only)
6. **Numbers over words**: "30 seconds" not "half a minute", "5 hairstyles" not "several options"

### Tagline Options

- Primary: "Your Personal Stylist" (current)
- Campaign: "One Photo. Infinite Style."
- Social: "AI knows your best look"
- Subscription: "Wake up styled"

### Copy Templates

**Social media post:**
> [Hook -- surprising or aspirational statement]
> [1-2 sentences of value/result]
> [CTA with link]

**Ad copy:**
> [Problem] + [Solution] + [Proof] + [CTA]
> Example: "Tired of bad hair days? AI finds your perfect hairstyle from one selfie. 4.8/5.0 satisfaction. Try it free."

**Email subject lines:**
- "Your AI stylist found 5 new looks for you"
- "[Name], your morning outfit is ready"
- "This hairstyle was made for your face shape"
